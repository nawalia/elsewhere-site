Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Every plan starts with a free trial, so you can check that Elsewhere works on your iPhone before you pay.

Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refundable. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Lifetime

You can ask for a full refund of a lifetime purchase within 14 days of buying it. After 14 days, lifetime purchases are not refundable.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. When a purchase is refunded, the access it gave ends and its code or licence key stops working. A chargeback made instead of asking us for a refund is treated the same way.

Nothing in this policy limits any refund right you have under the law where you live.

Help

For a refund or anything else, use Contact Nims in Settings > Help centre.
