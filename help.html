How it works

What is actually happening on my phone?

iOS has a location simulation tool that Apple built for developers testing apps. Normally a Mac drives it over a cable. Elsewhere drives it from the phone itself, over a connection that loops back into the phone, so no Mac is involved.

Is this a jailbreak?

No. Nothing is unlocked, patched or installed into iOS. You turn on a switch Apple put in Settings, and you can turn it off again whenever you want.

Does the location drift or snap back?

It should not. iOS forgets a simulated location the moment the connection closes, so Elsewhere holds the connection open, sends the same place again, and reconnects if it is ever interrupted. The coordinates are exactly the ones you picked and nothing is added to them.

How long does it hold?

As long as Elsewhere is running, including when the phone is locked. Restarting the phone always returns it to its real location, on purpose. Numbers for battery use and for very long holds will be published here once they have been measured properly, not estimated.

Why does it need a VPN switched on?

Apple's developer service inside iOS only answers connections that look as if they came from another device. The helper is a loopback tunnel: it turns the phone's connection back on itself and sends nothing to the internet. A version of Elsewhere that does this on its own, with no second app, is being built.

What other people and apps see

Will Find My show the place I set?

Yes. Anyone who can see your location in Find My, including family sharing, sees the place you picked, not where you are. Keep that in mind before you set one.

Can apps tell the location is simulated?

Some can. iOS attaches a flag to a simulated location and any app is free to look at it. Elsewhere does not hide that flag and will not add anything to hide it.

Does it change my IP address?

No. Elsewhere changes the location your phone reports, not the way it connects to the internet. A website can still see roughly where you are connecting from. If you want that changed too you need a real VPN, which is a different thing.

Can I use it for games?

Elsewhere is not built or sold for that. It does not hide the simulated flag, games often check it, and breaking a game's rules is between you and them.

Buying and keys

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. A purchase gives you a licence key that you paste in once, and it works on 2 iPhones.

I am getting a new iPhone

Free one of your two slots yourself from the link in your receipt, then paste the key on the new phone.

Does it need the internet?

Setting a location never does. A paid key is checked when there is a connection and keeps working for a month without one.

Can I get a refund?

If a lifetime plan does not work on your iPhone, yes, within 14 days. Monthly plans and day passes are not refundable, which is why there is a free trial. See the refund policy.
