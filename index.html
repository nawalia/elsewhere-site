Set a place

  • Tap, search or paste. Tap the map, search an address, or paste coordinates.
  • Satellite and 3D maps. Tilt the map and switch to satellite to pin the exact doorway.
  • Address book. Save places under your own names and set them again in two taps.
  • Back to real. One tap returns the phone to where it really is.

Move

  • Routes on real roads. Walk, run, cycle or drive between stops, following actual streets.
  • Drives like a car. Real acceleration and braking, slowing for corners, waiting at stops.
  • Joystick. Steer a set spot by hand; directions follow the way the map is turned.
  • Saved routes and GPX. Keep whole trips with waits at each stop, or import a GPX file.
  • Sat nav view. While a route plays the map tilts and turns with you.

Keep it there

  • Holds on by itself. iOS drops a simulated location when the connection closes; Elsewhere keeps it open and sets it again.
  • Set again in one tap. If the phone restarted or the app was closed, your last place is waiting on the map.
  • Alerts if it drops. You hear about it if your location stops being held, and a problem log keeps the history.
  • Checks it worked. Elsewhere watches what the phone itself reports and marks the place confirmed.

Control it from anywhere

  • Lock screen. A Live Activity shows the place and lets you pause or go back without unlocking.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Set a saved place or go back to real by voice or automation.
  • Help centre. Checks your setup step by step and tells you what to fix.
  • Make it yours. Light or dark, and five app icons to choose from.

Setup

Five minutes, once.

Elsewhere walks you through each step and checks it for you.

  1. Developer Mode

    A switch Apple puts in Settings. The phone restarts once. Not a jailbreak.

  2. Pair with itself

    Elsewhere shows a code, you type it into Settings. No Mac involved.

  3. Turn on the helper

    A free loopback app routes the connection back into the phone. One button opens it.

  4. Pick a place

    Tap the map, press Set, open Find My. You are there.

Straight answers

What it is not.

It is not a VPN

Elsewhere changes the location your iPhone reports, not your internet address. Websites can still see roughly where you connect from.

It does not hide that it is simulated

iOS marks a simulated location, and Elsewhere does not cover that up. Some apps check.

Find My viewers see the place you set

Anyone who can see your location sees the place you picked. What you do with that is up to you.

Restarting brings back your real location

On purpose. Then Set again puts you back in one tap.
