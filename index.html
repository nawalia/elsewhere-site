The idea

Your phone,
your location.

  1. Every iPhone already has the tool. Apple builds location simulation into iOS so developers can test apps in other places. It normally needs a Mac and a cable. Elsewhere drives it from the iPhone itself.
  2. iOS forgets a simulated place the moment the connection closes. So Elsewhere keeps the connection open, sets the place again whenever it slips, and tells you if it ever can't.
  3. Exact means exact. The coordinates you picked, to the metre. Nothing added to make it look natural, no drifting, no snapping back.
  4. Nothing about you leaves the phone. No account, no tracking, and the places you set are never sent anywhere.

Everything it does

One app.
Every way to be somewhere else.

Set a place

  • Tap, search or paste. Tap the map, search an address, or paste coordinates.
  • Satellite and 3D. Tilt the map and switch to satellite to pin the exact doorway.
  • Address book. Save places under your own names; set one in two taps.
  • Back to real. One tap and you are where you really are.

Move

  • Routes on real roads. Walk, run, cycle or drive between stops.
  • Drives like a car. Speeds up, brakes, slows for corners, waits at stops.
  • Joystick. Steer by hand; directions follow the way the map is turned.
  • Saved routes and GPX. Keep whole trips, or import a GPX file.

Keep it there

  • Holds by itself. Reconnects and sets the place again if the connection drops.
  • Set again in one tap. After a restart, your last place is waiting.
  • Alerts. You hear about it if your location stops being held.
  • Confirmed. Elsewhere checks what the phone reports and marks the place confirmed.

Control it anywhere

  • Lock screen. See the place and stop it without unlocking.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Set a saved place or go back by voice or automation.
  • Ask Nims in the app. Questions answered inside Elsewhere, no email needed.
  • Yours. Light or dark, and five app icons.

What broke along the way

Tried on my own iPhone.
A lot.

Plenty didn't work before something did. Here's the short version.

Didn't work

Talking to iOS directly

Wi-Fi, mobile data, the cable link, the phone's own loopback. iOS turned down every one.

Didn't work

A VPN from iOS's own settings

The phone sent the connection to itself and dropped it, because both ends looked the same.

Didn't work

A VPN on a free Apple account

Apple doesn't hand out that permission for free. The paid account does, and that version is ready.

Works

A loopback tunnel

The connection gets turned back into the phone. It connects in under a third of a second.

Works

The iPhone pairing with itself

On iOS 27 the phone pairs from Settings. No Mac needed, ever.

Straight answers

What it is not.

It is not a VPN

Elsewhere changes the location your iPhone reports, not your internet address. Websites can still see roughly where you connect from.

It does not hide that it is simulated

iOS marks a simulated location, and Elsewhere does not cover that up. Some apps check.

Find My viewers see the place you set

Anyone who can see your location sees the place you picked. What you do with that is up to you.

Restarting brings back your real location

On purpose. Then Set again puts you back in one tap.
