Install Elsewhere

You need an iPhone on iOS 17.4 or later. Setting it up takes about five minutes and you only do it once. Elsewhere checks each step for you and tells you if something is missing.

Elsewhere is in beta and the download is not open yet. Write to nanimswa@gmail.com and Nims will send you the beta. It goes on sale on 25 December 2026.

Setting it up

  1. Turn on Developer Mode

    On the iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, Developer Mode, switch it on. The phone restarts once and asks you to confirm after it comes back. This is a switch Apple puts in iOS. It does not jailbreak anything and you can turn it off again whenever you want.

  2. Pair the iPhone with itself

    Open Elsewhere and go to Setup. It shows a six digit code. In Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode, tap "Pair with Elsewhere" and type the code. This is the same pairing a Mac does when you plug a phone in, except there is no Mac and nothing leaves the phone.

  3. Turn on the connection helper

    Apple's developer service inside iOS only accepts connections that look as if they come from another device, so something has to route Elsewhere's connection back into the phone. Today that is a small free app called LocalDevVPN, which adds a loopback VPN. Elsewhere has a button that opens it. It carries nothing to the internet: it only turns the phone's connection back on itself.

  4. Pick a place and press Set

    Tap the map, search for an address, or paste coordinates. Press Set, wait for "Applied", then open Find My: the phone is there. It stays there until you press Stop or restart the phone.

Ways to install, and where they stand

Being honest about what works today and what is being built.

From the iPhone, with the helper

Works today. Elsewhere plus the free connection helper, both on the phone. No computer once you are set up.

From a Mac or PC over the cable

Being built. A small desktop app that installs Elsewhere with your own Apple ID and keeps it installed, for people who would rather not add the helper app.

Straight from this website

Being built. Once Elsewhere has its own Apple developer account you will install it from Safari, and the connection will be handled inside Elsewhere itself.

If something does not work

Setup says it cannot connect

Nine times out of ten the connection helper is switched off. Check Settings, VPN, and make sure it is on. Only one VPN can be on at a time, so another VPN will stop Elsewhere connecting.

"Pair with Elsewhere" does not appear in Settings

Developer Mode has to be on first, and Elsewhere has to be open and on the Setup screen while you look, because that is when it offers the pairing.

The location went back to where I really am

iOS drops a simulated location when the connection closes. Elsewhere holds the connection open and sets it again if it slips, so this normally means the helper was switched off or the phone restarted. Restarting always returns the phone to its real location, on purpose.

An app still shows my real location

A handful of apps ask iOS whether a location was simulated, and iOS tells them. Elsewhere does not hide that and will not. Those apps will keep using their own answer.
