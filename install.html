Where installing stands

Three ways in.

Apple only lets apps outside the App Store onto an iPhone in a few ways. These are the three Elsewhere is building, and how far along each one is.

01  Straight from this site

iPhone only, nothing else

Tap install in Safari and Elsewhere arrives, with its own connection built in, so no helper app either. The built-in connection is done; the install link needs Elsewhere's Apple developer account, which comes before launch.

02  From a Mac or PC

Your own Apple ID, a cable, once

A small desktop app installs Elsewhere signed with your own free Apple ID, and Elsewhere then keeps itself installed from the phone every week. Being built.

03  Beta testers

Installed with Nims

Works today, for the small group testing the beta. Join the list above and you are first in line when one of the two routes above opens.

Once it is installed

Setting it up.

About five minutes, once. Elsewhere checks each step for you.

  1. Developer Mode

    Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode. The phone restarts once. Not a jailbreak, and you can turn it off.

  2. Pair with itself

    Elsewhere shows a code. Type it into Settings, Developer Mode, "Pair with Elsewhere".

  3. The connection

    Switch on the connection Elsewhere asks for. It loops the phone's connection back into itself and sends nothing to the internet.

  4. Pick a place

    Tap the map and press Set. Open Find My: you are there.

If something goes wrong

Fixes.

Setup says it cannot connect

The connection is almost always switched off. Check Settings, VPN. Only one VPN can be on at a time.

"Pair with Elsewhere" is missing in Settings

Developer Mode has to be on, and Elsewhere has to be open on its Setup screen while you look.

My real location came back

The phone restarted or the connection was switched off. Open Elsewhere and tap Set again.

Still stuck

Open Elsewhere, Settings, Help centre, Ask Nims, and say which step you are on. Or write to nanimswa@gmail.com.
