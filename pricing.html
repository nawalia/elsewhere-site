What a purchase gives you

A licence key

Paste it into Elsewhere once. One key works on 2 iPhones, and you can free one up yourself if you change phone.

It works offline

Elsewhere checks your key when it can and keeps working for a month without any connection at all.

No account

No sign up, no password, no email needed to use it. Your email only goes to the payment provider so they can send the key and the receipt.

Refunds

If Elsewhere does not work on your iPhone, write to Nims and you get your money back within 14 days of buying a lifetime plan. Monthly and day passes are not refundable, so use the free trial first to check it works for you. The full terms are on the refund policy page.

Payments are taken by Polar, who are the seller of record and handle the tax.
