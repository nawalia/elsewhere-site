Uses

What it's for.

Elsewhere started as a tool for one job: testing apps in places you aren't. It turned out that the same thing, an iPhone that reports exactly the place you choose and keeps reporting it, is useful to a lot more people. Here is all of it, in plain words first and then the technical side.

Why it exists

The purpose.

  1. Your location is yours. Dozens of apps on a phone ask where you are, many all the time. Elsewhere gives you the say over what they are told.
  2. The tool already existed, locked behind a Mac. Apple built location simulation into every iPhone for developers. Elsewhere makes it something anyone can use from the phone itself.
  3. It had to be dependable. A location that drifts or snaps back is worse than none. Holding a place for hours, and saying so honestly when it can't, is the whole point.

For everyone

Simple uses.

Privacy

Keep your home address out of apps

Weather, shopping, social and dating apps all want your location. Give them your town, not your street.

Safety

Share a place you are comfortable sharing

Meeting someone new, at a big event, travelling alone: decide what your phone says about where you are.

Travel

Plan a trip from home

See local search results, restaurants, events, weather and time the way people there see them, before you go.

Moving

Get to know a new city

Look around the neighbourhood you are moving to with apps that show what is near you.

Content

Local results, wherever you are

Search, maps and recommendation apps that tailor results by place show you another place's version.

Fun

Walk somewhere you have never been

Set a route through Tokyo or Paris and watch your phone walk it, at real walking speed.

For work

Professional uses.

App developers

Test location features on a real device

Geofences, region monitoring, store finders, delivery zones, local pricing, time zone handling. No Mac or Xcode session needed while you test.

QA testers

Repeatable routes

Save a route with waits at each stop and replay it exactly, at walking, cycling or driving speed, as many times as a test needs.

Designers and marketers

Screenshots from anywhere

Capture an app as it looks in another city or country for a pitch, a case study or store screenshots.

Sales and demos

Show a client their city

Demo a location-based product as if you were standing in the client's town.

Researchers

Study how apps treat location

See which apps check whether a location is simulated, and what they do about it.

Students

Learn how iOS location works

Watch Core Location, Find My and Live Activities respond to a location you control.

Technical

How it works.

For the curious, and for developers deciding whether it fits their testing.

The mechanism
Apple's developer location simulation the same service Xcode uses, reached through the iPhone's developer services
Connection
A loopback tunnel on the phone iOS only accepts developer connections that look as if they come from another device, so the connection is routed back into the phone
Pairing
The iPhone pairs with itself on iOS 27, from Settings, Developer Mode; on earlier versions a Mac pairs once
Holding
A hold loop iOS clears a simulated location when its connection closes; Elsewhere keeps it open, sends the place again and reconnects with backoff
Confirmation
Checked against Core Location the place counts as confirmed once the phone's own reported fix matches it
Routes
Real roads from Apple Maps with acceleration and braking near 2 m/s², sideways limits near 3 m/s², corners rounded within 15 m
Formats
Coordinates, addresses, GPX decimal degrees pasted from anywhere, and GPX files from other tools
Engine
Rust, with a Swift app on top the engine runs on the iPhone itself
Simulated flag
Left as iOS sets it apps that ask whether a location is simulated get the true answer
Data
Stays on the phone places, routes and the pairing never leave it; the pairing is kept in the Keychain

Not for

What it won't do.

Fool the people in your life

Anyone who sees your location in Find My sees the place you set. Elsewhere is not built or sold for misleading partners, family or employers.

Cheat in games

It does not hide that a location is simulated, and games that check will know. Breaking a game's rules is between you and them.

Change your internet address

It is not a VPN. Websites can still see roughly where you connect from.
